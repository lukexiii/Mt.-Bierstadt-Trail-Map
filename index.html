Mt. Bierstadt

Mt. Bierstadt is one of Colorado's 53 official 14ers (mountains with a summit elevation over 14,000 feet). It is located in the Front Range and is one of the closest 14ers to Denver, next to Mt. Blue Sky, which it is connected to via the Sawtooth Ridge.

Mt. Bierstadt is widely considered one of the easier 14ers to summit, as it can be a great entry into the 14er hiking hobby. However, it is still a difficult and demanding hike that is over 7 miles long and has almost 3,000 feet of elevation gain. Near the summit, there is a ~400 ft. class 2/3 scramble, but it is well-marked with cairns and markings.
